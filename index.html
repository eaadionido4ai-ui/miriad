MIRIAD is Monitoring system and Incident / outbreak Reporting for Infectious Animal Diseases. Built for raisers and animal health officers to detect, log, and contain, even with no signal.

OFFLINE-FIRSTASFAIFMDRABIESLSDPCIC READY
Abstract

When an animal disease hits, the first hours decide everything. But field reporting in the Philippines still stalls where signal drops, and the raisers and officers closest to the animals are the ones left without tools.

MIRIAD puts outbreak response in the pocket. Officers run premises registry, containment zoning, and situation reports. Raisers keep a herd record that captures each animal the way PCIC livestock insurance requires it, tracks policy expiry and animal status, and turns a mortality into a Notice of Loss inside the ten day window the policy allows. Everything is encrypted on the device and syncs when a connection appears.
